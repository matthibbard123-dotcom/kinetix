How Kinetix works.

Start with the information you have. Add more as you play and review.

01

Choose your game

Select a game in your Kinetix dashboard and set up its player profile.

02

Add your gameplay

Enter match results, log a session, or provide a gameplay clip for review. Supported integrations may add data automatically.

03

Review your stats

See your recorded performance over time and spot patterns in your results.

04

Learn with your AI coach

Ask for practical feedback based on the stats and gameplay material you provide.
